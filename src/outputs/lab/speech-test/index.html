On-device speech test

A test bench, not a product. It checks whether a small Whisper model can turn spoken tasks into text on a real phone, fast enough and without a network.

Privacy on this page. The model and its code are downloaded once from Hugging Face and jsDelivr, then kept in this browser. Your recordings are processed in this tab and never uploaded. Results are saved only in this browser until you copy them.
