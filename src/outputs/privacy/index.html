PuneStack Tools / Privacy

Where your data goes

Plain answers, checked against the code. Last reviewed 8 November 2026.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, and no PuneStack server that receives what you enter.
  • Your entries are saved in this browser’s local storage on this device. Clearing your browser data deletes them.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

The exceptions

SituationWhat leaves your device
You tap a microphone buttonYour browser’s speech service receives the audio to turn it into text. In Chrome and Edge that is Google or Microsoft, under their privacy terms. Type instead if nothing should leave your device.
You tap Share or ExportOnly what you chose, sent to the app or file you picked.
Loading the websiteOur host, Vercel, receives standard request details (such as your IP address and browser) to deliver the pages.
Speech test lab pageDownloads a speech model from Hugging Face and code from jsDelivr once, then runs on your device. Your audio is not uploaded. This page is only for testing.

Delete your PuneStack Tools data

Removes every task, recap, expense, audit and brief that PuneStack Tools saved in this browser. This cannot be undone.
