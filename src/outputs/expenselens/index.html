ExpenseLens: Keep receipts organised for your CA.

Shop owners and small offices that hand a bag of receipts to their accountant every month.

Try the preview

The problem

Receipts fade, go missing, or sit in a drawer until the CA asks for them. Typing them into a sheet at month-end takes hours.

What works in the preview today

  • Enter vendor, amount, date, category and GSTIN for each receipt
  • See this month’s total at a glance
  • Export a CSV for your CA

Not built yet

  • No receipt reading (OCR). You type each value yourself
  • A GSTIN format check does not confirm registration or input-tax-credit eligibility
  • Not a GST return or accounting system. Your CA must review every entry

Where your data goes

No account and no PuneStack server. Here is exactly what happens to what you enter.

WhatWhere it goes
Expenses you enterStay in this browser. PuneStack never receives them.
Receipt photoOnly its file name is shown; the image is not read, stored or uploaded in this preview.
CSV exportSaved by your browser to your device.
