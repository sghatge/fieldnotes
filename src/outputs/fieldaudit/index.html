Early preview

FieldAudit: Site checklists that work without a network.

Site supervisors, store managers and food-safety leads who inspect places with weak or no signal.

Try the preview

The problem

Paper checklists get lost, and most inspection apps stop working in basements, warehouses and remote sites.

What works in the preview today

  • Three ready checklists: site safety, retail opening and food safety
  • Tick items and add findings by typing or dictation
  • Save the latest audit and share a summary
  • Tick-box checklists work offline after the first visit

Not built yet

  • No photos and no sync to a team
  • No custom checklists yet
  • No on-device speech model. Dictation needs your browser’s speech service and a connection

Where your data goes

No account and no PuneStack server. Here is exactly what happens to what you enter.

WhatWhere it goes
Checks and findingsStay in this browser. PuneStack never receives them.
Dictated findingsYour browser’s speech service receives the audio (Google in Chrome, Microsoft in Edge).
