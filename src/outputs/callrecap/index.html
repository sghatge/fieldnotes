CallRecap: Every client call becomes a clean follow-up note.

Brokers and sales people who make many short client calls from the car or the shop floor.

Try the preview

The problem

After a call you remember the mood but not the details. Follow-ups slip, and CRM entries never get written.

What works in the preview today

  • Type or dictate notes right after the call
  • A simple recap pulls out a summary, any objection and the next step using keyword rules
  • Copy the recap for your CRM or WhatsApp

Not built yet

  • It does not record calls. Planned as a voice memo after the call, not call recording
  • No speaker separation and no on-device speech model
  • No CRM integration

Where your data goes

No account and no Fieldnotes server. Here is exactly what happens to what you enter.

WhatWhere it goes
Typed notes and recapsStay in this browser. Fieldnotes never receives them.
Dictated notesYour browser’s speech service receives the audio (Google in Chrome, Microsoft in Edge).
