VoiceTask: Your to-do list listens.

Freelancers and shop owners who remember jobs while moving between customers, sites and calls.

Try the previewWant to join the pilot? Tell the person who shared this link with you.

The problem

Tasks arrive while your hands are busy. By the time you sit down, half of them are gone. Notes apps take too many taps, and typing on the move is slow.

What works in the preview today

  • Type or dictate a task in one line
  • A due date is suggested from words like “tomorrow”, “next week” or “by Friday”, and you confirm or change it
  • Tick tasks off, undo, and share open tasks to WhatsApp
  • Works offline for typed entry after the first visit; installable to your home screen

Not built yet

  • No on-device speech model yet. The mic uses your browser’s speech service
  • Date suggestions understand simple English phrases only. Hindi and Marathi phrases are not supported yet
  • No reminders or notifications
  • No sync between phones; tasks live in one browser

Where your data goes

No account and no PuneStack server. Here is exactly what happens to what you enter.

WhatWhere it goes
Typed taskStays in this browser (local storage). PuneStack never receives it.
Dictated taskYour browser’s speech service turns audio into text. In Chrome and Edge that means Google or Microsoft receives the audio.
Shared listOnly when you tap Share, and only to the app you pick.
